The Technology

Built like critical infrastructure. Because it is.

Every gun, cabinet and transformer on the WattSquare network is connected, monitored and power-managed in real time.

Dynamic power sharing

The site's full capacity is pooled, not split evenly. Each vehicle draws the fastest rate it can safely accept, and spare power moves instantly to whoever needs it.

Live uptime monitoring

Every gun reports its own health continuously. A fault takes the unit off the map before anyone drives to it — stopping the single biggest driver frustration.

AI route planning

Give it a start, destination and current charge. It places the stops, estimates arrival, and routes you around busy stations across 10,000+ partner chargers.

One-tap payments

Scan the QR and pay by UPI, card or RFID. No app download, no membership required. The price per unit is on the screen before a single unit flows.

Fleet-grade dashboard

Per-vehicle and per-driver consumption, cost centres, session history and exportable reports — with monthly credit billing on a single invoice.

Every connector, every vehicle

CCS2, Bharat DC-001 and Type 2 AC on the same forecourt, with bay geometry and cable reach planned for intercity coaches and trucks — not just hatchbacks.

How it works

Three steps. No app required.
